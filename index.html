Recruiter

How to move up

Deliver steady results, look after every candidate and learn from your Senior Recruiter.

2

Senior Recruiter

What you do

Handles harder searches, owns client relationships and coaches newer recruiters.

How to move up

Show you can guide others, not only deliver your own work.

3

Team Leader

What you do

Guides a small team, sets daily priorities and clears blockers.

How to move up

Build a team that delivers on its own and keeps its standards high.

What you do

Leads several team leaders and keeps quality consistent across accounts.

How to move up

Prove you can run more than one team and grow other leaders.

5

Unit Manager

What you do

Runs a full unit, including its targets, hiring and growth.

How to move up

Show strong judgement, trusted results and a clear view of the company’s direction.

6

Orbit CEO

What you do

Sets the direction of the company and the standard for how we treat people.
